# PlaceLoadHeld

`PlaceLoad`, with the value held against all the places (`HoldsAt.mono` from the places older than the
home). The shape of an eval-position read of a homed local or of a place through one (lowering-spec S6b,
4, and leafC's (b) and (c)). The `Homes` comes from P3's invariant, or from `LentHomes.homes`.
